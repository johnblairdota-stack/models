## What these clips actually look like (watched, not paraphrased)

1. **One hit removes a section.** Hole is body-sized or plank-length, not a nibble. Edges are stair-step cubes because the world *is* voxels — packed flush until they break.
2. **Pieces that leave are islands**, not a grid of identical cubes. A table stays a table while it falls. A wall sheds slabs and lumpy clusters. A timber plank stays a long rectangle in flight (`yt-ign-52s`).
3. **Tumble is readable.** Rotation + motion blur. You can follow one chunk from wound to floor (`yt-house-240s`, `yt-ign-62s`).
4. **They land and stay.** Heap has structure: slabs at angles, leaning, mixed sizes (`yt-ign-65s`, `yt-house-68s`).
5. **Skin ≠ interior.** Painted/wood face vs grey meat at the cut (`yt-house-148s`, `yt-house-240s`).
6. **Dust is behind the slabs**, never instead of them (`yt-house-95s`, `yt-ign-37s`).
7. **Unsupported mass falls as one.** `yt-house-145s` is the island rule in one frame.

## Vs our smash lab (`s-table-round-wound.png`)

Our floor pile is a **uniform field of small white cubes**. Teardown's pile is mixed island sizes, original surface colour on the outside of chunks, and a wound that looks carved from a solid, not a dotted grid.

Copy: section size, island tumble, persist, skin/meat. Do not copy "every piece is the same 8cm cube."
