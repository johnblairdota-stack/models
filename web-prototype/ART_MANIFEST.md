## UNIT-4H spec, read directly off sheet 04 (+ 07/08/09/10/17 for detail)

**Proportions** — heroic-cute, ~6.5 heads tall. Big rounded head, compact torso, slim
limbs, oversized rounded boots. Read off the front view as fractions of total height:

| Landmark | Fraction of height |
|---|---|
| Top of head | 1.00 |
| Chin / neck joint | 0.845 |
| Shoulder pivot | 0.815 |
| Chest decal centre | 0.755 |
| Waist (narrowest) | 0.655 |
| Hip pivot / pelvis disc centre | 0.545 |
| Fingertips at rest | 0.415 |
| Knee pivot | 0.285 |
| Ankle | 0.055 |
| Sole | 0.00 |

**Shoulder-to-shoulder silhouette = 0.375 of height.** Measured off the front figure of
the turnaround sheet with a hand-placed crop (crown y=107 to sole y=721, H = 614 px),
mint-cap outer edge to mint-cap outer edge. The mint cap itself is 0.0765 wide × 0.0912
tall, spanning 0.780→0.691 of height.

> An earlier version of this file said **0.245**, which was wrong — eyeballed, not
> measured. It did real damage: a builder spent a 462k-token run narrowing the shoulders
> to hit it, which made the piece score *lower* than before the rebuild, and a critic then
> had to catch it. Automated measurement also failed three times (returning 0.212, 0.35
> and 0.095) because the robot is near-white `#EDEFF0` on a near-white cyc `#F2F2F2`: a
> luminance threshold finds only dark inner structure, a background-delta threshold catches
> the contact shadow and the sheet title, and the figure's widest point overall is at the
> *hips* with the arms hanging, not the shoulders.
>
> **The method that worked: crop the front figure by hand first, then measure inside that
> crop.** Use it for any other dimension in this table you need to trust. Head is ~0.155 tall, ~0.135 wide.
Boot length ≈ 0.115. Hand ≈ 0.075 long with **four fingers + opposed thumb**, each
finger visibly 3-segmented.

**Head.** Rounded-cube helmet, wider than deep, crown slightly domed. Front is one
**dark blue-glass faceplate** — a rounded-rect "screen" set flush, on which the face is
drawn as emissive light: two soft rounded rectangles for eyes and a single thin upward
crescent for the mouth, all a lighter cyan-blue than the glass. **No physical mouth.**
A **circular ear disc** on each side, concentric rings, chrome, like a speaker. Thin
panel-line seam runs over the crown front-to-back. Rear of skull has a small vent
louvre group, lower left.

**Torso.** Two-piece: an upper **chest shell** (white, glossy, one large soft-cornered
plate with a shallow central sternum ridge and a parting line under each pec) and a
lower **abdomen** cluster of 3 stacked chrome segments that narrow to the waist. The
**4Humanity split-head mark is printed on the wearer's left pec** (viewer's right on the
front view), small, ~0.055 of height, in muted blue-grey — a printed decal, low contrast,
not emissive. Back has a single large curved spine plate with a vertical seam and a
small louvre stack at the small of the back.

**Shoulders.** THE signature read: a **mint-green rounded cap** over each shoulder, a
soft asymmetric teardrop shell, glossy, that hoods the top of the arm. Under it, a
**chrome ball joint** with a visible ring collar. Mint is matte-satin, noticeably less
reflective than the white shells.

**Arms.** Chrome-satin. Upper arm is a tapered cylinder; elbow is a **stacked-disc hinge**
(3–4 thin chrome rings); forearm slightly fatter at the elbow, tapering to a **squared
wrist block with a small dark inset panel** on the outer face. Hands are chrome, fingers
slim with dark knuckle gaps.

**Pelvis.** A white saddle block with a chrome belt band, and on each side a **large
concentric-ring drive disc** (three rings, dark centre bore) — the hip ball housing.
These discs are big, ~0.09 of height, and unmistakable. Sheet 08 shows them best.

**Legs.** White outer thigh shell over chrome inner structure; **knee is a ring disc**
matching the hips at ~0.6 scale, with a white kneecap plate over it on the front view.
Shin is white-shelled with a chrome ankle stack. **Boots** are big, soft, rounded — a
white upper with a distinct sole break, a chrome ankle collar, toe slightly upswept, and
a dark rubber sole edge.

**Materials, in the sheet's own values**

| Surface | Colour | Finish |
|---|---|---|
| Primary shells (chest, thigh, boot, helmet) | near-white `#EDEFF0` | glossy clearcoat, roughness ~0.18, faint warm bounce |
| Structural / limbs / joints | chrome-satin `#B9BEC2` | metal, roughness ~0.28, anisotropic-ish highlight |
| Shoulder caps | mint `#8FC9BD` | satin, roughness ~0.42, non-metal |
| Faceplate glass | deep blue `#2C5C93` | smooth glass, roughness ~0.08, dark |
| Face light (eyes, mouth) | cyan-blue `#6FA8E8` | emissive, soft-edged, slight bloom |
| Panel gaps / knuckle gaps / sole edge | `#2A2E31` | matte |
| Chest decal | `#5C7286` | printed, flat |

Lighting in every sheet: soft high-key studio on a **near-white cyc** `#F2F2F2`, one
large soft key from front-upper-left, a broad fill, and a rim from behind. Contact
shadow directly under the feet only. That's the *sheet* lighting — the game is
Alien: Isolation dark. Only the **materials and the model** transfer.

> almost-clean shell** with one faint smudge on the back — not the red eyes and dulled
> `#DDD8D2` this table asserts. I wrote the table from an overall impression of the sheet
> rather than from that row, and a builder then matched the table, which is why stage 1
> renders visibly sootier than the art it is copying.
>
> **Stage 1 is the player chassis, barely touched — blue eyes, near-clean.** The horror is
> that it is recognisably one of you. The red eyes and the grime are what stage 2 brings.
> That also restores the ramp's headroom, which the current build has spent early.
>
> Second spec error found in this file (the first was shoulder width). When this table and
> the art disagree, **the art is the bar** — measure the art, do not trust the table.
