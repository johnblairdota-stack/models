THE NIGHT

Eight phones are controllers. One TV is the show. One server decides who may know what. One mansion is the set. They are not four features — they are one product, and it only works when they move as one.

Pitch why the coupling is the product

The guide taps a door on a private map. The server stores one pin and tells exactly three parties: the TV (as a control cue it may never draw), the runner's bezel, and the guide's own pad reading it back off the wire. The body auto-walks that pin one door at a time — never the true target, because the lie is the game. The thumb is only a dodge. HOLD hides behind furniture, and there is no hiding in an open hall. The camera cranes at the ballroom threshold and never cuts under a thumb. The hunter is a door that is shut.

The rule of this board: every claim names the gate that proves it, and a DONE card must name a gate that runs in gates:party. night-coupling NC10 reads this very file and goes red if a DONE card tries to skip verify.
