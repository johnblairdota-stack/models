Who now knows something

  • The Continuity holderone private reading an episode — did the announced pair contain Production? The first of thirteen role cards that is not just text. Their dossier names the pair, so it is still spendable in episode 5.
  • Their double, sometimesa Glitched dealt a Continuity cover reads the same card and gets the exact inverse on every entry. 22.6% of eight-player games. Neither of them is told.
  • The guide, for 90 secondsthe flyover is the only private frame surface in the game, and it is operational rather than social — it expires when the expedition does
  • Everyone else: nothingmeasured, not asserted — party-private, six complete games on both servers

What the room can now check

  • Whether the guide could seeblind and wrong is unlucky; sighted and wrong is something else. The honest-error mechanic — 62° tilt, a 2.55 m blind strip, a decaying last-known — finally legible.
  • Who sent themspecced in rrr-paper-prototype.md §2 and never built. Episode 1 stops after DEBRIEF, so this is the only evidence it can leave.
  • The arithmetic that killed someonethe record was rowed all, projected to every socket, and rendered on no screen until today
  • None of it is proofand that is the design — two channels can make a reading honestly wrong, and neither is visible to its holder

What the loop still lacks

  • Twelve of thirteen cards are still textContinuity fires. The Camera Op, the Focus Puller, the Editor, the Producer's spike and the rest do not.
  • The Producer has no lever at allnoise.spike is a declared event kind emitted by nothing — yet party-sim models it 85% of episodes, so every balance number ever quoted here is from a different game
  • The vote is still near chance28.4% of executions hit Production against a 26.4% baseline. Today's two evidence channels are not in that number — the sim cannot see them.
