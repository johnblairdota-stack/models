Reading the desk

Two encodings, and they are independent. The ring is how loud it was — loudness × 14 m, the range the Hunter itself hears at. The core is how well you know where: a dot inside camera coverage, a band of bearing and range outside it. A room with a camera in it sits a shade lighter and carries a lens.
Placed
A camera has that room. The mark sits on the metre and carries the room's name.
Bearing only
No camera. A band across the plan, usually two rooms wide, carrying no name at all.
