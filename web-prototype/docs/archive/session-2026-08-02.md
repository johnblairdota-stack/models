## The routing lesson, measured

- Every agent asked to **diagnose or design** returned a permanent win.
- Every round where the lead **converted a verbal defect into geometry numbers** was roughly a
  coin flip. **Four of those specs were provably wrong**, one internally contradictory, two
  caused score regressions.
- Builders caught all four, because every brief said *"if a stated fact is wrong, say so rather
  than diverging silently."* **That line is the highest-yield sentence in the process.** Seven
  agents in a row used it and all seven were right.

**Corollary: stop writing numbers you cannot see the result of.** Give outcomes plus design
authority, and say which is which.

## Instrument failures — the tools lie too

Three separate times an instrument returned a confident wrong answer:

- an image-diff helper **froze**, returning identical numbers three times, because Chromium
  caches `file://` by URL and every render writes the same path. It did not error;
- the `envMapIntensity` ablation measured **a disconnected uniform**;
- **element-existence AND `isVisible()` both passed** on content rendering underneath a
  `z-index:100` splash. Only a screenshot caught it.

**Rules that came out of it:** cache-bust or use `data:` URLs; a probe that cannot observe must
report SKIP, never PASS; and if you assert something is on screen, have looked at a picture of it.
